# Milestone 5.1, pilot readiness: summary for the owner's review (2026-11-26)

> **Status: accepted by the owner on 2026-11-26.** The owner approved the milestone on 2026-11-24
> ([`2026-11-24-pilot-readiness.md`](2026-11-24-pilot-readiness.md)). This summary says what was built, what was

## The objective, and where it stands

**The objective:** one QA team repeatedly uses the first customer flow on one authorized staging application, with
synthetic data and one dedicated test account, and trusts the verdict.

**Where it stands:** everything engineering can do without outside decisions is done.
- **Waiting on the owner:** the rehearsal on a real host (hosting) and pilot invitations (an email provider).
- **Waiting on API credit:** the rest of the measurement.

## Added during the milestone, beyond its items

These were found or asked for while building:
- **Defects found and fixed:**
  - an unreachable site is reported as such, not as a failure (the owner's decision F5);
  - the key-ring canary (R-20);
  - one data call at a time on self-refreshing pages.
- **The egress proxy:**
  - it now judges every request (R-22);
  - a refused WebSocket no longer counts as an unreachable site;
  - it is a second layer for guarded runs on http (R-23).
- **Exploring client-side applications** (ADR-0021 amendment): fragment routes, reading a page once drawn, stopping
  in time.
- **Stronger locators** (ADR-0042): fields by label, menus and tabs, landmarks and dialogs, and proof that a locator is
  the element's own.
- **Plans recorded:**
  - the data architecture (ADR-0038, accepted);
  - the owner's application-understanding goal;
  - the Enterprise programme E1–E10;
  - a daily stand-up.

## What was measured, with its limits

- **Release candidate (R5, `v0.5.0-rc.1`):** 7 of 12 unseen runs, no false positive. Incomplete, because the provider
  failed and then credit ran out.
- **Coverage on applications nobody here wrote** (no model; the share of hand-written baseline steps the exploration
  shows):

  | Application | Acting | Read-only | Guarded pressing |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Juice Shop (tuning) | 74% | 68% | 74% |
  | Gitea (clean) | 73% | 68% | 68% |

- **Our three applications:** no coverage figure fell through any change in the milestone.
- **Not yet measured:**
  - **every planner figure predates the prompt of ADR-0042**;
  - no planner has run on Juice Shop or Gitea.
