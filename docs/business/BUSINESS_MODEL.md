# Business model

> Status: **working hypotheses**, accepted as the direction on 2026-11-15. Nothing here is a
> commercial commitment or a published price. Engineering gates are in
> [`../commercial-readiness.md`](../commercial-readiness.md). Product scope is ADR-0020.

## The thesis
A software company reduces its QA headcount by using Autestiq across the QA journey. The product
starts with AI-generated end-to-end UI tests and expands to other kinds of testing. Every QA artifact
is generated by AI from recorded evidence.

**Claim policy.** The thesis is tested, not asserted. Until customers have measured time saved on
their own work, the product says it *helps* QA teams and never that it *replaces* them. Time saved
is measured as the directive defines it (§2.2, §12): not estimated engineering hours.

## Who pays
- **Primary:** small SaaS engineering teams (about 3–20 developers), with GitHub workflows and limited
  QA capacity.
- **Secondary:** software agencies with several client projects.
- **Later:** mid-market and enterprise. Not prioritised before the core product is validated.

## Expansion order
1. AI-generated E2E UI tests of a web application (first product).
2. API testing.
3. Performance testing.
4. Database testing.
5. Security and penetration testing, each with its own threat model.
6. Hardware testing. A long-term vision, not designed or scheduled.

Each step opens only when the previous one meets its measured gate (ADR-0020).

## Pricing hypotheses (unvalidated)
From the directive §5.2, preserved as hypotheses. Values stay configurable and are not published until
validated against measured cost per run.

| Plan | Price (€/month) | Browser minutes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Developer (free) | 0 | 100 | Strict quotas, abuse protection |
| Starter | 99 | 500 | Up to 3 projects |
| Growth | 399 | 2,500 | Primary offering; up to 10 projects |
| Scale | 1,299 | 10,000 | Higher concurrency; fair-use limits on projects |
| Enterprise | custom | custom | Only what is built |

Annual billing hypothesis: 15% discount. Billable failures follow the decision in
`commercial-readiness.md` §9, option B.

**Margin check before any price.** Gross margin is a target (75–80%), not a fact. The price of a
browser minute, a model call and storage must be measured first (gaps G10–G11).

## Validation, in order
1. **Product gate:** the planner meets the published benchmark gate (ADR-0020).
2. **Customer discovery:** 20 qualified interviews (directive §17).
3. **Design partners:** 3–5 teams using the product on a real staging environment.
4. **Paid pilots:** at least two paying pilots.
5. **Repeated use:** organizations returning across development cycles.

Revenue targets (€2,000, then €10,000 MRR) and "five paying companies" are measured outcomes, not
software acceptance tests.

## Non-goals for now
- Native mobile or desktop testing; full penetration-testing capability.
- Autonomous changes to customer applications.
- Enterprise compliance workflows, SSO and data residency beyond what is already designed.
- Custom payment processing. Billing is Stripe in test mode first, after the ledger is correct.
